## Context

At **Kilimax**, a company backed by Transsion, I worked as an AI Community Agent from July to October 2026. The team was investing in advertising but couldn't clearly see **where the money went**: how much each step cost, and when customers gave up.

## What I built

An end-to-end **monitoring funnel**, from the **ad click** to the **paid conversion**:

- a Python **data pipeline** that gathers each step's events;
- the **cost per step**, to know what every dirham spent brings back;
- **drop-off detection**: the step where most customers leave;
- **alerts** when a metric degrades, and **data quality** checks;
- a **dashboard** for the team, with a summary view: where customers come from, and where we lose the most, across all channels.

## Beyond the funnel

I also built a **product-market fit** (PMF) model with market research for Morocco, designed a **go-to-market** strategy and reached out to influencers for partnerships.

## What I took away

Data only matters if someone can act on it. The most useful piece wasn't the most complex model, but a simple view answering one precise question: **where do we lose our customers, and what does it cost?**
